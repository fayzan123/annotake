How it works

Nothing you record leaves your Mac.

Plain files
Each recording is a folder of PNGs and a steps.md in ~/Pictures/Annotake. They clear out after 30 days unless you choose to keep them.
No AI
Frames are picked by comparing pixels, and captions come from macOS Accessibility. No models, no uploads.
Only while you record
Annotake sees your screen only while you record, and it never records what you type.
